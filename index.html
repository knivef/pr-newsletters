New Feature

Introducing Operator Mode

Anyone can now become a payment facilitator with PayRam. Operator Mode lets you deploy a PayRam instance on your own server, onboard merchants under your platform, and charge custom processing fees. There is no paperwork, no setup costs, and no middlemen involved.

Whether you are a SaaS platform looking to monetize payments, a development agency building embedded finance into client products, or any business that wants to offer payment processing as a service, Operator Mode gets you there without the usual compliance overhead or gateway negotiations.

How it works
  • 1️⃣Deploy a PayRam instance on your server
  • 2️⃣Select Operator Mode during setup and complete configuration
  • 3️⃣Start onboarding merchant tenants
  • 4️⃣Set custom fees per merchant (up to 15%)
  • 5️⃣Each merchant gets a dedicated dashboard with their own login
What your merchants get
  • 🖥️A dedicated storefront dashboard
  • 💳Fiat and crypto payment processing out of the box
  • 🔒Clean separation from other tenants on your platform

There is no cost to get started. Deploy, configure, and begin onboarding merchants on day one.
